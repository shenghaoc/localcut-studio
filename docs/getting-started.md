# Getting Started

## 1. Import media

Click **+** in the Media panel (or use the Media menu) and choose one or more video/audio files. Each file appears in the bin with a thumbnail and duration. Files stay where they are on disk — LocalCut Studio references them; it doesn't copy them.

## 2. Build a timeline

Double-click a media item (or right-click → **Add to Timeline**) to append it. Video goes on a **V** track and audio on an **A** track. Clips are added one after another at the end of the timeline.

## 3. Preview

The preview shows your timeline composited in real time.

- **Play / Pause** — Space, or the transport button.
- **Scrub** — drag in the time ruler at the top of the timeline.
- **Go to start** — the ⏮ button.

The red line is the **playhead**; its time is shown in the transport bar alongside the total duration.

## 4. Edit

- **Select** a clip by clicking it.
- **Split** the selected clip at the playhead — the **Split** toolbar button (scissors).
- **Delete** the selected clip — the **Delete** toolbar button or the Delete key.
- **Opacity** — with a clip selected, use the Inspector's Colour/Clip controls.

## 5. Project settings

In the Inspector's **Project** section, choose a **Resolution** (1080p, 720p, 4K, or vertical) and **Frame Rate** (24/30/60). These define the output canvas for both preview and export.

## 6. Export

Click **Export**, choose a destination, and LocalCut Studio renders a `.mov` of your timeline. A progress indicator shows export status; the status line confirms completion.

## Tips

- Zoom the timeline with the slider in the timeline header (or the ± glyphs).
- What you see in the preview is exactly what you get on export — they share one render pipeline.
